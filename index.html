02 / Research
RESEARCH // 01 — PUBLISHED (IJMSM)

Applications of AI and IoT to Develop Intelligent Car Parking Systems in Hanoi, Vietnam

Authors: Lam An Binh, Le Ba Duc, Nguyen Do Anh Dung, Duong Tuan Dung, Nguyen Anh Kiet, Vu Xuan Manh Journal: International Journal of Multidisciplinary on Science and Management (ISSN: 3048-5037) Published: May 26, 2026 (Vol. 3, Issue 2, pp. 163-168) DOI: 10.71141/30485037/V3I2P119

This research addresses Hanoi's severe traffic congestion and parking shortage, where current facilities meet only 8-10% of total demand. The paper proposes an Intelligent Car Parking System (ICPS) integrating IoT and AI technologies. Key implementations include ultrasonic and geomagnetic sensors for real-time occupancy tracking, cloud database integration, and computer vision algorithms (OpenCV) for automated license plate recognition (ANPR) and security surveillance.
